[ Solution ]

NCR, Diebold Nixdorf, Nautilus Hyosung, OKI, GRG, and other devices can work within one ATM network. Sollisero ATM creates a common environment for them with no need to restructure the software framework when a new vendor’s hardware is installed. You can upgrade and expand your ATM fleet making channel development independent of any single vendor’s hardware platform.

Unified ATM software built on the Windows XFS platform. Successfully implemented at Bereke Bank (Kazakhstan) and more than 50 other organizations.
