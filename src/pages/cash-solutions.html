[ 02 ] The problem02 / 12

One sale.
Many systems behind it

Every sale touches the cash register, the payment, stock and accounting. When these live apart, the counter slows down and the merchant spends time reconciling sales, stock and price tags by hand.

Cash registerManual reconciliationSales
POS terminalSeparate historyTransactions
Stock countTakes several daysPrice tags
Disconnected processPoint of sale

01Slow checkout

Finding products and taking payment by hand holds up the queue at the counter.

02Scattered sales data

Sales, stock and price tags are tracked separately, so there is no single view of the business.

03Inventory takes days

Counting stock can take several days and pulls staff away from customers.

[ Solution ]

cash solutions connects every sale to payment, stock and accounting, so the counter keeps moving and the merchant no longer reconciles data by hand.

Everything sits in one merchant portal, from a single receipt to the day’s results.
