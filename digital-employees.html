[ 02 ] THE PROBLEM02 / 12

Dozens of repetitive operations a day slow processes down

Specialists re-check information, search for answers, re-type data into systems and cross-check documents by hand. Each operation takes minutes — together they take hours every day and pull people away from decisions, customers and non-standard tasks.

A SPECIALIST’S DAY · TYPICAL SPLIT
Clarifying and searching for information~40%
Re-typing data into CRM / ERP / Service Desk~35%
Cross-checking documents and templates~25%

Routine first, real work second. digital employees reverses the order.

01Customer requests

Managers re-check information, search for answers and copy data into the CRM by hand.

02Technical support

Engineers spend time collecting initial information and sorting out routine incidents.

03Documents and proposals

Specialists manually cross-check data, verify completeness and fill in templates.

04Internal processes

Employees look for instructions, collect task statuses and ask colleagues for details.

[ SOLUTION ]

digital employees takes over the repetitive operations and hands people a prepared result — leaving them control, decisions and the non-standard tasks.

Not an assistant that suggests — a digital executor that works inside the process, within your rules and access rights.

[ 04 ] HOW IT WORKS04 / 12

Not just an AI assistant. A digital executor inside the process.

A typical AI assistant helps find information or draft a reply. digital employees builds a process in which a digital employee receives a task, works with corporate data, performs permitted actions and hands over a finished result.

01

Receives the request

From chat, email, a web form or the Service Desk.

→
02

Studies the context

Knowledge base, dialogue history and data in corporate systems.

→
03

Performs operations

Only permitted actions in CRM, ERP and other systems.

→
04

Prepares the result

A document, a reply, a record or a task.

→
05

Hands over for review

To a person or straight into the work system.

EXAMPLE

Proposal request

How one digital employee runs the process end to end.

CUSTOMER

A customer sends a request with the items they need.

→
digital employees

Checks prices and stock in the ERP, calculates the cost and prepares a draft commercial proposal.

→
MANAGER

Checks the terms and sends the proposal to the customer.

All actions follow defined rules and access rights. Significant decisions stay under human control.
