Manage accounts and cards, send transfers by phone or card number, pay bills, track spending by category, search transaction history, get statements and chat with the bank.

Instant transfers by phone number, card number and between own accounts, including to other banks. Utilities, mobile top-ups, taxes and fines are paid from saved templates or by scanning a QR code, and payments can be scheduled.

Balance, spending and withdrawal limits, freeze, PIN change, issuance and replacement — all available to customers in the app, without a branch visit.

Face ID / Touch ID, OTP by phone, and login and password changes online. Customers choose the sign-in method and which events they get push notifications about.
